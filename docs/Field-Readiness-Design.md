# Field-Readiness Curriculum Design

**Status:** Design and implementation record  
**Scope:** Practical crop-advisory capability, capstone work, localisation, and portfolio evidence  
**Version:** 1.0

## Programme position

The field-readiness layer is additional evidence of practical development. It does **not** replace the current sequential pathway, module assessments, 80% pass mark, final certification rules, or owner-notification rules. It requires learners to combine observation, uncertainty, recommendation, communication, economics, safety, and review into traceable advisory work.

## Algeria-relevant learning anchors

| Anchor | Curriculum implication | Localisation boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Water productivity and water stress | Every practicum and capstone must quantify the water question, source, delivery condition, root-zone evidence, and review trigger. Digital plot-level evidence is treated as a professional method, not an optional add-on. | Do not transfer irrigation duration, rate, or scheduling between farms; learners must use local soil, crop stage, source, and system evidence. |
| Seasonal variability | Every crop-calendar decision must include a forecast check, a weather-linked risk, and a contingency response. | The platform provides a calendar-building method, not a national fixed planting calendar. Learners validate the current local window with local extension, weather, buyer, and farm records. |
| Market and cost uncertainty | A recommendation must identify the buyer or household objective, cost, labour, expected return, sensitivity, and a condition for revision. | Price, yield, and input values are learner-entered local assumptions; the platform does not supply a guaranteed price, profit, or investment recommendation. |
| Food safety and crop-protection governance | Harvest, post-harvest, and pesticide work must include label, registration, interval, hygiene, water, and traceability checks. | Legal and label information must be checked against the current official product label and competent Algerian authority source before action. The course is educational and does not issue legal approval or product recommendations. |

FAO identifies severe water stress as a central Algerian agricultural challenge and highlights precision and plot-level water-resource management capacity as a priority [1]. Its June 2026 country brief also illustrates why a field-ready course needs weather-responsive planning: dry early-season conditions delayed planting in western areas, followed by rainfall-driven recovery, while fresh fruit and vegetable prices changed sharply year on year [2]. A 2022 regulatory overview records food-safety, plant-protection-label, and seed/seedling controls, but explicitly notes that requirements can change; therefore, the course teaches a **current-source verification workflow** rather than fixed legal claims [3].

## Required field-readiness evidence

| Evidence | Learner demonstration | Quality standard |
|---|---|---|
| Field advisory practicum | Records visit verification or supervision status, farm context, grower interview notes, observations, competing explanations, provisional diagnosis, recommendation, communication, and follow-up outcome. | Separates fact from report and inference; includes a referral or escalation boundary where competence or evidence is limited. |
| Integrated capstone | Resolves a multi-factor farm case with agronomy, water, nutrition, crop protection, economics, climate, and review logic. | Names the limiting factor, alternatives rejected, safety condition, cost assumption, and verification step. |
| Local intelligence card | Builds a locality-specific crop and risk profile from farm, extension, weather, market, and regulatory sources. | Records source date, uncertainty, and the condition that requires update. |
| Digital evidence | Uses a field map or zone description, dated observations, photo or measurement references where lawful and consented, and traceable records. | Protects grower confidentiality and never presents unverified evidence as a diagnosis. |
| Professional reflection | Reviews decision quality, grower communication, ethical boundary, and revision trigger. | Identifies a concrete improvement and the evidence that would change the recommendation. |

## Structured rubric self-review

Practicum and capstone work use the same six criterion-level self-review dimensions: **evidence quality**, **uncertainty and decision boundary**, **safe practical recommendation**, **local intelligence and economics**, **communication and ethics**, and **digital record and follow-up**. Learners select the lowest honest level from “not yet” to “defensible” for each criterion, then add a written reflection. The rubric is developmental rather than an external grade; it helps a learner identify what must improve before a supervisor, specialist, or grower review.

## Learner-facing resources

The Field Readiness Portfolio includes a five-step local-intelligence method—place, season, market, safety, and review—and dedicated learning cards for farm economics, adviser communication and confidentiality, and digital field methods. These resources make the localisation boundary practical: a learner must use current, dated local information rather than treating course content as a fixed national calendar, guaranteed market price, or legal approval.

## Validation record

The learner navigation exposes the **Field Ready** entry point. Visual route checks confirmed the protected practicum workspace presents fifteen evidence fields, including visit verification, grower interview notes, provisional diagnosis, and follow-up outcome, with confidentiality and referral guidance. The same route now displays all six criterion-level rubric controls. The capstone workspace presents five selectable integrated cases with evidence packs, required decisions, substantive response prompts, written reflection, the same structured rubric, and private save controls. The field-readiness hub uses protected learner overview data, displays local-intelligence and professional-method resources, and retains an explicit non-gating notice.
