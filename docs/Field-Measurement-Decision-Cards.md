# Field Measurement and Decision Cards

**Status:** Implemented as an open learner resource from Field Ready and the relevant source-derived modules.  
**Purpose:** Convert high-consequence source content into repeatable routines: prepare, measure or observe, record, decide, review, and stop/test/refer.

## Design boundary

The cards strengthen field judgement; they do **not** provide fixed fertiliser or pesticide rates, certify water, approve a product, replace a laboratory result, or state a current legal requirement. Each card explicitly requires current label, laboratory, extension, official-source, or specialist verification where the uploaded-file content does not establish a safe conclusion.

| Routine | Reinforced source modules | Core evidence | Bounded decision standard |
|---|---|---|---|
| Representative soil-sample chain | 02, 16 | Zone, depth, crop context, history, field observation, label | Use results only for the represented zone; resample or refer if sample integrity is uncertain. |
| Root-zone moisture and air check | 23 | Rooting depth, moisture, aeration, irrigation, rain, drainage, crop stage | Change irrigation only with root-zone evidence; inspect again after the relevant water event. |
| Water-quality screen | 23–25 | Source, intended use, visible condition, contamination pathways, filtration | A visual screen is not a laboratory result; test or refer where risk or intended use is high consequence. |
| Drip delivery and uniformity check | 25 | Source, filter, near/far-end flow, wetting pattern, leak, maintenance history | Trace the water path before extending run time or replacing parts. |
| Nutrient-plan evidence and 4R check | 13, 26 | Soil/pH context, crop stage, source analysis, roots, prior use, placement | Correct the limiting factor; do not infer a universal rate from a symptom. |
| Sprayer pre-use and calibration check | 33 | Current label, crop/target fit, weather, PPE, water, nozzle, equipment, calibration | Stop work when safe, lawful, or calibrated application cannot be verified. |

## Learner access

The **Field Readiness Portfolio** links to the cards as six open routines. Modules 02/16, 13/26, 23, 24/25, and 33 expose their relevant card link in the learner-support column—even when the current lesson is locked—so that a learner has a clear, non-gating reference route.

## Validation standard

Regression coverage verifies the exact six-card set, source-module mappings, preparation/observation/record/decision structure, review and referral content, water and pesticide routing, and unknown-card fallback. Visual validation confirms the full card workspace and module-side links for Water Management and Responsible Use of Pesticides.
