# Module 5: Cost and Uncertainty Planning Source Notes

This short internal note records the external material used to strengthen Module 5 and its voluntary plan-revision scenario. It teaches transparent assumptions and uncertainty-aware planning; it does not provide an enterprise budget, financial projection, supplier endorsement, or financial advice for a learner’s own farm.

| Source | Design-relevant principle used in Module 5 | URL |
| --- | --- | --- |
| University of Florida IFAS Extension, *Getting Started with Enterprise Budgets and Sensitivity Analysis* | Enterprise budgets depend on the quality of their inputs; sensitivity analysis helps explore how price, cost, and yield changes can affect a projected outcome. | https://ask.ifas.ufl.edu/publication/FE1175 |
| Oklahoma State University Extension, *Using Enterprise Budgets in Farm Financial Planning* | Enterprise budgets set out the production system, inputs, sequence of operations, costs, and returns; representative budgets must be tailored to the individual operation. | https://extension.okstate.edu/fact-sheets/using-enterprise-budgets-in-farm-financial-planning |
| Food and Agriculture Organization, *Farm Management and Marketing Manual* | Gross margins are a planning building block and should be interpreted with attention to uncertainty and the underlying assumptions. | https://openknowledge.fao.org/bitstreams/8a03e0b8-c118-44b8-b7f7-950f5f74d663/download |

The implementation therefore separates quantities, sources, quote dates, terms, and assumptions; uses comparison and sensitivity questions rather than a predicted outcome; and makes plan revision or a qualified business-support referral a valid response to weak evidence.
