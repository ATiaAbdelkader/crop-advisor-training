# Printable Quantified Scouting Sheet

**Status:** Implemented as a public, print-ready A4 landscape resource from the Quantified Scouting Protocol and Modules 29–32 and 34.  
**Purpose:** Give learners a compact blank form for recording a repeatable sample, field-pattern evidence, incidence and severity reasoning, pests and beneficials, decision rationale, recheck, and referral conditions in the field.

## Sheet structure

| Section | Included field evidence | Why it is retained |
|---|---|---|
| Scouting setup | Date/time, field or zone, crop and stage, scout, question, route/unit, number examined | Makes a later count interpretable and repeatable. |
| Quantified observations | Zone, plant part/pattern, pest/symptom/sign/weed evidence, sampled/affected count, incidence/severity, beneficials/traps/host weeds, contributing conditions, decision/recheck/referral | Keeps observation, inference, pressure, ecosystem evidence, and next action distinct. |
| Decision review | Evidence supported, uncertainty, proportionate option, verification needed before escalation, same-route recheck, referral condition | Prevents a count or symptom from being treated as an automatic recommendation. |
| Boundary | No diagnosis confirmation, universal threshold, intervention prescription, or replacement for local rules, labels, or specialist advice | Maintains the source-grounded, non-prescriptive safety standard. |

## Use and download

Learners can open the **Quantified Scouting Protocol** and select **Print blank scouting sheet**, or open the linked support card from Field Diagnosis, Disease, Insect and Mite, IPM, or Weed Management and select **Print blank scouting sheet**. The print command is deliberately labelled **“Print / save as PDF”** so the browser’s print workflow can either produce paper copies or save a PDF without requiring a separate file service.

The sheet does not alter the sequential curriculum, 80% module checks, certification, or owner-alert rules. It is an open support resource and remains available from the linked module pages while lessons are gated.

## Print standard

The sheet uses A4 landscape print settings with reduced margins, screen-only navigation hidden during printing, a five-row observation table, and print-safe section boundaries. It is designed for handwriting rather than data entry; digital record storage remains available separately through the existing IPM field record.

## Validation standard

Regression coverage verifies the stable sheet identifier, six setup fields, eight observation columns, four use steps, three decision-review prompts, five valid source-module links, incidence/severity and beneficial-evidence fields, and the non-prescriptive boundary. Visual validation confirms the print-ready route, protocol entry point, and linked IPM module support card.
