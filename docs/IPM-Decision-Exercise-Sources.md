# Integrated Pest Management Decision Exercise: Design Sources

| Source | Design implication |
|---|---|
| [FAO, *How to practice Integrated Pest Management*](https://www.fao.org/agriculture/crops/thematic-sitemap/theme/spi/scpi-home/managing-ecosystems/integrated-pest-management/ipm-how/en/) | An IPM decision needs regular field observation, crop and ecosystem context, pest and natural-enemy awareness, and consideration of more than one factor. |
| [United States Environmental Protection Agency, *Integrated Pest Management Principles*](https://www.epa.gov/safepestcontrol/integrated-pest-management-ipm-principles) | Prevention, monitoring, accurate identification, locally established thresholds, and evaluation of lower-risk options occur before any control choice; sighting a single organism is not an action decision. |

The Module 32 exercise will organise prevention, evidence, uncertainty, and escalation. It will not establish thresholds, name products, prescribe controls, or give pesticide directions.
