# Module 4: Farm-Context Planning Source Notes

This short internal note records the external material used to strengthen Module 4 and its voluntary crop-selection evidence-gap scenario. It supports context-aware learning and does not establish crop, variety, water, fertility, market, legal, or investment advice for any locality.

| Source | Design-relevant principle used in Module 4 | URL |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Texas A&M AgriLife Extension, *Key Factors in Vegetable Production* | Planning should consider site, water supply and quality, crop and variety selection, and market development together. | https://aggie-horticulture.tamu.edu/vegetable/guides/organic-vegetable-production-guide/key-factors-in-vegetable-production/ |
| Rutgers Cooperative Extension, *Site Selection Considerations for New and Expanding Farms* | Site analysis should inventory farm goals, field history, soil, water, drainage, access, and operational resources before a production decision. | https://njaes.rutgers.edu/fs1342/ |
| Food and Agriculture Organization, *Extension and Advisory Services* | Advisory services support locally responsive, evidence-based decisions across complex farm and value-chain contexts. | https://www.fao.org/research-extension-systems/extension-and-advisory-services/en |

The implementation uses a farm-context sequence that makes evidence gaps explicit and directs high-consequence choices to current local, authorised sources or qualified support.
