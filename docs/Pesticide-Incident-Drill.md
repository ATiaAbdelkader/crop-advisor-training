# Pesticide Incident Drill

**Status:** Implemented as an open learner-support resource from Field Ready and Modules 23, 32, and 33.  
**Purpose:** Train a safe decision sequence for a pesticide incident, exposure concern, release, equipment fault, or near miss: recognise and stop, protect people and water, use label-led escalation, then document and prevent recurrence.

## Safety and scope boundary

The drill is an **escalation and evidence resource**, not a substitute for current emergency, medical, environmental, legal, employer, product-label, supplier, or safety-data-sheet instructions. It deliberately does not provide medical treatment, first-aid details, spill-cleanup technique, disposal directions, regulatory thresholds, or local reporting rules. Learners must use the current product label, safety information, and appropriate authorised local channels for the actual product and incident.

| Drill stage | Reinforced source modules | Learner evidence | Required boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Recognise and stop | 32, 33 | Task, product-identity status, visible fault or concern, persons and area affected | Stop unsafe work and do not improvise a product, remedy, cleanup, or restart. |
| Protect people, water, and area | 23, 33 | Water/drainage pathway, sensitive areas, weather, wind, access control, equipment condition | Use current label and authorised procedures; the course does not prescribe containment or cleanup. |
| Label-led escalation | 33 | Current label/safety information, product details as shown, channel contacted, facts reported | Do not diagnose, treat, or rely on memorised product/legal information; use authorised emergency or medical channels where required. |
| Document, review, and prevent recurrence | 29, 32, 33 | Chronology, observed facts, notifications, uncertainty, corrective action and verification date | Keep factual records, protect confidentiality, and do not replace an official investigation or report. |

## Learner access

The **Field Readiness Portfolio** links to four open safety stages. Module 33 displays all four stages; Module 32 displays recognition and review stages; Module 23 displays the people-and-water protection stage. These support cards remain visible while a lesson is gated, but they do not change the existing sequential assessment or 80% pass requirements.

## Validation standard

Regression coverage verifies the exact four-stage set, complete immediate/protection/record structure, source-module mappings, explicit boundary and follow-up language, responsible-pesticide/IPM/water access, and unknown-stage fallback. Visual validation confirms the drill workspace, Responsible Use of Pesticides, Integrated Pest Management, Water Management, and Field Ready routes.
