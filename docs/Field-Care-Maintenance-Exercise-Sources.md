# Field-Care Maintenance Evidence Exercise: Design Sources

| Source | Design implication |
|---|---|
| [FAO, *Farmer Field School Implementation Guide*](https://openknowledge.fao.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/1f5c22af-a438-4573-8667-ec14d5233c7e/content) | Routine observation should record crop growth, pest/disease clues, soil, and field conditions before a learner prioritises an issue or selects a next evidence step. |
| [University of Minnesota Extension, *Recordkeeping for specialty crops*](https://extension.umn.edu/agriculture/farm-operations-and-systems/agricultural-business-management/marketing-farm-products/recordkeeping-for-specialty-crops) | Focused, consistent records support later comparison and farm decisions; the exercise therefore uses a short, traceable field-maintenance note. |

The Module 27 exercise will develop observation and prioritisation discipline. It will not recommend a treatment, product, rate, threshold, or management schedule.
