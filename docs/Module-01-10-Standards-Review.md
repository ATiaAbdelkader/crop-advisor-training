# Module 01–10 Standards Review

## Review purpose

This review checks the first ten modules as a connected agricultural-learning sequence. It evaluates the learner-facing definition rather than judging a module only by its title or completion status. The intended result is a source-grounded, adult-appropriate learning path that moves from advisory process and observation to production planning and environmental diagnosis.

| Criterion | Review evidence | Standard for acceptance |
|---|---|---|
| Source grounding | Lesson language, field brief, source notes, and curriculum theme | Content retains the decision context and practical safeguards established in the uploaded training source. |
| Instructional depth | Two ordered lessons, summaries, section content, and outcomes | The module explains both the concept and the field reasoning required to use it; it does not rely on unsupported shortcuts. |
| Applied practice | Field-brief context, task, evidence, and quality standard | Learners can identify a realistic field action, record appropriate evidence, and judge whether the action was completed well. |
| Assessment alignment | Four scored module questions, feedback, and linked final competency | The check tests a material decision or diagnostic judgement, and the final competency uses a corresponding source-theme anchor. |
| Accessibility and progression | Ordered lessons, 80% pass mark, learner-safe wording, and visible feedback | Requirements are understandable, appropriately gated, and usable without depending on an image alone. |
| Visual integration | Module-specific asset, alternative description, learning caption, and fallback state | The image reinforces the active lesson, has a meaningful description, and does not block learning if it is unavailable. |

## Audit method

The review inspects the canonical curriculum, applied field-brief map, semantic assessment-alignment map, and visual map for Modules 01–10. It then checks the learner routes and existing regression suite. Findings are classified as **meets standard**, **refinement recommended**, or **requires correction**. Any correction will be implemented in the canonical content definitions and retested before the review is closed.

## Findings and refinements

The initial structural review found that Modules 04–10 already met the complete applied-learning standard introduced during the document-derived curriculum upgrade. They each had two sequenced lessons, a four-item 80% assessment, an applied field brief, semantic linkage to a final competency, accessible visual treatment, and a learner-safe visual fallback.

Modules 01–03 had equally substantial lessons and visuals, but their previous foundation-module design used three-item checks and did not yet include the newer applied field-brief or semantic-alignment artifacts. This was a standards-consistency refinement rather than a source-grounding failure. The modules were brought to the same standard by adding an applied field brief, a fourth decision-focused assessment item, and explicit assessment-to-final-competency alignment.

| Module | Focus of review | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| 01 — Advisory practice | Evidence sequence, risk-aware records, and decision follow-up | **Meets standard after refinement.** Added an auditable follow-up assessment item, field recommendation brief, and final-competency anchor. |
| 02 — Soil and nutrition | Root-zone context, representative sampling, and cautious nutrient decisions | **Meets standard after refinement.** Added a context-before-fertiliser assessment item, sampling field brief, and final-competency anchor. |
| 03 — Crop observation | Growth-stage scouting, variability diagnosis, and uncertainty | **Meets standard after refinement.** Added a repeatable scouting-record item, field brief, and final-competency anchor. |
| 04 — Vegetable production planning | Site appraisal and whole-system planning | **Meets standard.** Rich-picture planning is carried from lesson to field brief, assessment, and final competency. |
| 05 — Cost planning and decisions | Quantities, costs, returns, and revision | **Meets standard.** The applied brief and final item require a decision when assumptions do not support a credible return. |
| 06 — Crop and variety selection | Rotation, local adaptation, and market fit | **Meets standard.** Learners must weigh buyer requirements against locally defensible variety performance. |
| 07 — Factors affecting crop yield | Genetics and interacting environmental limits | **Meets standard.** The module avoids yield promises and requires a limiting-factor analysis. |
| 08 — Climatic factors and crop yield | Crop-stage climate risk and response | **Meets standard.** Learners connect a climate signal to stage-specific risk, action, and review. |
| 09 — Topographic factors and crop yield | Elevation, slope, runoff, and layout | **Meets standard.** The module requires a topographic observation to become a placement, drainage, or erosion-control decision. |
| 10 — Edaphic soil factors and crop yield | Physical, chemical, and biological soil interpretation | **Meets standard.** Learners integrate multiple soil lenses rather than prescribe from a single indicator. |

## Validation record

The updated canonical curriculum now provides four questions at the 80% pass mark for every Module 01–10 check. Each module has an applied field brief, an accessible source-aligned instructional visual, and semantic evidence linking the module check to the related final-assessment competency. The project typecheck, regression suite, and production build passed after the refinements; the suite contains 43 tests.

Learner-route captures confirmed that Modules 01–03 render their instructional visuals and retain the sequential lesson and assessment gates after the change. Module 01 and Module 02 were recaptured after the development-server restart and rendered their live visual, lesson context, and 80% standard correctly; Module 03 retained its visual and locked progression treatment. The existing verified visual coverage remains in force for Modules 04–10.

> **Review conclusion:** Modules 01–10 now apply a consistent standard of evidence-led teaching, field practice, assessment feedback, competency alignment, accessibility, and visual support. No source-grounding, assessment, or learner-safety correction remains open within this review scope.
