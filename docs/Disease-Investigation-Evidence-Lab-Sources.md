# Disease Investigation Evidence Lab: Design Sources

| Source | Design implication |
|---|---|
| [Pacific Northwest Pest Management Handbooks, *Disease Diagnosis and Management*](https://pnwhandbooks.org/plantdisease/diagnosis-testing/disease-diagnosis-management) | Learners should define the problem, inspect field and plant-part patterns, investigate time development, distinguish symptoms from physical signs, and build a case history before narrowing explanations. Photo matching alone is not reliable for difficult cases. |
| [University of Florida IFAS, *Sample Submission Guide for Plant Diagnostic Clinics*](https://ask.ifas.ufl.edu/publication/SR007) | Authorised diagnostic referrals require representative material and sufficient case history; local clinic instructions should determine handling and submission. |

The Module 30 lab will be an evidence and referral exercise. It will not identify a disease, verify a regulated concern, recommend pesticides or other interventions, or direct disposal, reporting, or movement restrictions.
