# Nursery-to-Stand Quality Pack

**Status:** Implemented as a learner-facing, non-gating resource from Field Ready and Modules 17–22.  
**Purpose:** Build a continuous quality system from seed and nursery batch planning through daily nursery care, transplant acceptance, early stand counts, and follow-up investigation.

## Design boundary

The pack translates the uploaded nursery and transplanting content into four repeatable field routines. It does **not** declare a seed batch viable, diagnose plant disease, certify water or media, prescribe a crop-specific treatment, or override current specialist, extension, label, or supplier advice. Its core habit is to identify evidence, accept or hold the next operation, record the decision, and revisit the outcome.

| Routine | Reinforced source modules | Quality evidence | Accept, hold, or refer standard |
|---|---|---|---|
| Seed and nursery-batch traceability | 17, 20 | Seed/lot, storage or treatment, crop/variety, field geometry, plant number, medium, batch identifier | Hold when seed, media, replacement capacity, or batch-to-field link cannot be verified. |
| Daily nursery hygiene and environment check | 17–19 | Drainage, sanitation, spacing, media moisture, cover/ventilation, uniformity, roots, pests, weather | Correct verified conditions before treating a symptom; refer persistent decline or uncertain crop-health risk. |
| Hardening and transplant-acceptance decision | 18, 19, 22 | Root and shoot condition, true leaves, root block, tray moisture, hardening, field readiness, weather | Accept only healthy, uniform, hardened seedlings matched to a ready field; hold uncertain or stressed groups. |
| Transplant execution and early stand recovery | 21, 22 | Holes, mulch, supports, moisture, nutrient separation, root handling, stand counts, zone pattern | Pause when field and plant readiness do not align; investigate early gaps before repeating inputs or replacements broadly. |

## Learner access

The **Field Readiness Portfolio** links to the pack as four open quality routines. Modules 17–20 and 22 display the relevant routine in their learner-support column even when the current lesson is locked. This lets a learner consult a safe operating routine without bypassing the course’s sequential assessments or 80% threshold.

## Validation standard

Regression coverage verifies the exact four-routine set, complete preparation/inspection/accept-or-hold/record structure, follow-up and referral content, source-module mappings, nursery and transplant route links, and missing-routine fallback. Visual validation confirms the full Quality Pack workspace, Nursery for Vegetable Production, Transplanting, and Field Ready routes.
