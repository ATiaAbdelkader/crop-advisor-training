# Pesticide-Use Readiness Exercise: Design Sources

| Source | Design implication |
|---|---|
| [WHO and FAO, *Guidance on good labelling practice for pesticides*](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240053014) | A current product label communicates authorised uses, hazards, and precautionary information, but effective risk reduction also requires complementary information and training. |
| [FAO, *International Code of Conduct on Pesticide Management*](https://openknowledge.fao.org/handle/20.500.14283/cc8321en) | Pesticide stewardship should be framed around shared responsibility and risk reduction throughout the product life cycle. |

The Module 33 exercise will support label verification, problem escalation, and decision deferral. It will not reproduce application, mixing, PPE, storage, clean-up, disposal, medical, regulatory, or reporting directions.
