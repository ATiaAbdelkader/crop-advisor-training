# Integrated Weed Observation Exercise: Design Sources

| Source | Design implication |
|---|---|
| [FAO, *Integrated Weed Management*](https://www.fao.org/agriculture/crops/thematic-sitemap/theme/spi/scpi-home/managing-ecosystems/integrated-weed-management/en/) | The exercise treats weed pressure as a crop-system observation involving competition for resources, field patterns, and possible pest or pathogen context. |
| [University of California Agriculture and Natural Resources, *Integrated Weed Management*](https://ipm.ucanr.edu/agriculture/pear/integrated-weed-management/) | Prevention, monitoring, and context-specific management are treated as linked components; local crop and system guidance remains necessary. |

The final Module 34 exercise will record field evidence, prevention context, identification limits, and escalation. It will not prescribe herbicides, physical operations, timing, rates, or thresholds.
