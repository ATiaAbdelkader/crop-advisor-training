# Module 11–20 Standards Review

## Review purpose

This review evaluates Modules 11–20 as a connected soil-to-seedling sequence. The modules cover soil degradation, plant nutrition, nutrient management, acid-soil management, soil health, soil sampling, nursery systems, open-field seedlings, protective cellular seedlings, and seedling-production planning.

| Criterion | Review evidence | Standard for acceptance |
|---|---|---|
| Source grounding | Canonical lessons, applied field brief, assessment, and linked final competency | The learning sequence retains the source document’s agricultural decision context, safeguards, and practical limits. |
| Instructional depth | Two ordered lessons, outcomes, and explanatory sections | Learners receive both the technical concept and the reasoning required to apply it under field conditions. |
| Applied practice | Field-brief context, task, evidence, and quality standard | Each module translates technical knowledge into a recordable farm or nursery action. |
| Assessment alignment | Four scored questions, feedback, and semantic final-competency link | The assessment tests a material management or diagnostic decision, while the final assessment uses a corresponding source theme. |
| Accessibility and progression | Ordered lessons, 80% pass mark, feedback, and sequential gate | Learner expectations and mastery requirements are explicit, usable, and appropriately gated. |
| Visual-support readiness | Textual field context, visual metadata status, and learner fallback behaviour | Content remains complete without imagery. Module 11–20 image generation is intentionally cancelled in this scope at the user’s request. |

## Audit method

The review checks the canonical curriculum, applied field-brief map, and semantic assessment-alignment map for Modules 11–20. It treats the absence of Module 11–20 imagery as an approved scope constraint rather than a content defect: the core explanation, evidence cues, field brief, and assessment must remain sufficient without visual media.

## Findings

All ten modules meet the reviewed content standard without requiring a curriculum change. Each has two ordered lessons with three explanatory sections, a four-question module check at the 80% pass mark, an applied field brief with a context, task, evidence record, and quality standard, plus a semantic source-theme connection to its related final-assessment competency.

| Module | Focus of review | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| 11 — Soil degradation and management | Erosion, salinity, pollution, and linked protection measures | **Meets standard.** Learners identify degradation evidence, match the control to the risk, and set a follow-up observation. |
| 12 — Nutrients required in plant nutrition | Essential nutrients, mobility clues, and cautious interpretation | **Meets standard.** The module distinguishes plant nutrition from automatic fertiliser use and requires root-zone evidence. |
| 13 — Nutrient management | Nutrient imbalance, removal, loss, and integrated planning | **Meets standard.** The module moves from symptom clue to a plan that supplies, retains, recycles, and protects nutrients. |
| 14 — Acid soil causes and management | Measured pH, acidification, liming, and complementary practice | **Meets standard.** The module rejects universal lime rates and requires measured soil and crop context. |
| 15 — How to promote soil health | Living soil, organic matter, crop rotation, and whole-system practice | **Meets standard.** The field brief uses reinforcing practices rather than treating one input as a complete solution. |
| 16 — Collect soil samples for soil testing | Zone definition, sample integrity, labelling, and traceability | **Meets standard.** Learners keep different decision areas separate and preserve field context from sample to laboratory. |
| 17 — Nursery for vegetable production | Root quality, spacing, protection, drainage, and insect exclusion | **Meets standard.** The readiness standard connects root and shoot quality to planned field conditions. |
| 18 — Open-field seedling production | Raised sites, thin sowing, cover management, hardening, and low-stress transplanting | **Meets standard.** The module protects roots and spacing across the full nursery-to-field sequence. |
| 19 — Protective and cellular seedling production | Cellular media, protected germination, hardening, and transplant readiness | **Meets standard.** The module makes field readiness a quality judgement rather than a calendar date. |
| 20 — Seedling production planning | Seed traceability, replacement allowance, media fit, and production constraints | **Meets standard.** The plan traces seed and media needs back to field geometry and establishment capacity. |

## Visual-support conclusion

Modules 11–20 do not currently have generated instructional images because the user cancelled that image-generation scope. This does not reduce the reviewed content quality: the lesson summaries, outcomes, field notes, applied field briefs, assessment feedback, and sequential progression remain complete and accessible without image-dependent meaning. If visuals are resumed later, they should follow the Module 01–10 pattern of a source-aligned image, specific alternative description, concise learning caption, and learner-safe fallback.

> **Review conclusion:** Modules 11–20 meet the source-grounded, adult-learning, applied-practice, assessment, competency, accessibility, and progression requirements. No content correction is required in this review scope.
