# Field-Diagnosis Evidence Board: Design Sources

| Source | Design implication |
|---|---|
| [Penn State Extension, *Solving the Case of Crop Disorders: The Role of Patterns in Diagnosis*](https://extension.psu.edu/solving-the-case-of-crop-disorders-the-role-of-patterns-in-diagnosis) | Field pattern, progression, affected species, and visible signs can shape competing explanations, but should not be treated as a final diagnosis. |
| [Kansas State University Plant Disease Diagnostic Lab, *Field Crop Samples*](https://www.plantpath.k-state.edu/extension/plant-disease-diagnostic-lab/collection-guidelines/field-crops.html) | Good referrals need field history, timing, extent, crop variety, weather/water/soil context, application history, healthy-versus-affected comparison, and field-pattern photos or representative samples. |

The planned board will organise competing explanations and identify the next evidence or referral route. It will not identify a pathogen, recommend a product, or provide pesticide, treatment, disposal, or reporting directions.
