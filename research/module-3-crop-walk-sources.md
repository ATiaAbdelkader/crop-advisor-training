# Module 3: Crop-Walk Evidence Source Notes

This short internal note records the external material used to strengthen Module 3 and its voluntary conflicting-evidence scenario. It supports evidence-first learning and does not establish diagnosis, treatment, threshold, product, or rate guidance.

| Source | Design-relevant principle used in Module 3 | URL |
| --- | --- | --- |
| University of Wisconsin–Madison Extension, *Introduction to Crop Scouting* | Effective scouting combines preparation, grower communication, systematic field observation, accurate documentation, and clear reporting. | https://cropsandsoils.extension.wisc.edu/articles/introduction-to-crop-scouting/ |
| Pacific Northwest Plant Disease Management Handbook, *Disease Diagnosis and Management* | Diagnosis should use field patterns, plant and root observations, time development, and case history; a photograph or isolated plant part may be insufficient. | https://pnwhandbooks.org/plantdisease/diagnosis-testing/disease-diagnosis-management |
| CABI, *Plantwise Diagnostic Field Guide* | Agricultural advisors need structured field support because no individual can be an expert on every crop and plant-health problem. | https://www.cabi.org/cabi-publications/plantwise-diagnostic-field-guide/ |

The implementation therefore uses a repeatable route, reference/typical/unusual comparisons, separate observation and interpretation fields, and a bounded recheck or qualified-referral option. The root-zone comparison record and scenario explicitly avoid input prescriptions.
