# Supervisor Competency Scoring

**Author:** Manus AI  
**Scope:** Private learner evidence and administrator review for every Crop Advisor Training module.

## Purpose and assessment boundary

The scoring workflow adds a structured developmental review alongside the programme’s established 80% formal assessments. Competency-based education places emphasis on a learner demonstrating specific knowledge, skills, and attitudes in authentic contexts, with transparent standards and progression based on the stated competence.[1] This workflow therefore asks learners to submit a completed module performance task, context, and review or referral boundary; it does **not** infer competence from time spent, a photograph alone, or a multiple-choice score alone.

| Participant | What they can do | What they cannot do |
|---|---|---|
| Learner | Submit private evidence for one module; see their own scorecard, status, and feedback. | View another learner’s evidence or alter a supervisor score. |
| Administrator supervisor | View the private queue; read module criteria and learner evidence; score and comment. | Change formal scores, sequential gates, certification, owner alerts, or issue unsupported crop-protection instructions. |

## Three-level scorecard

The supervisor scores each field-performance level separately using **Not yet evidenced**, **Developing**, or **Demonstrated**. The three levels are drawn from the course competency framework rather than from a universal agronomic threshold.

| Competency level | Supervisor review question | Expected evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Prepare** | Did the learner frame the task, method, needed evidence, and safety conditions? | Clear decision/task context, method, and evidence plan. |
| **Perform** | Did the learner complete the observable task or decision sequence with traceable evidence? | Field note, record, measurement, scenario response, practicum, capstone, or other module-aligned evidence. |
| **Review and refer** | Did the learner name a follow-up indicator, uncertainty, and condition to revise, stop, or refer? | Recheck or outcome plan, uncertainty statement, and authorised escalation boundary. |

Written feedback must identify the strongest evidence, any material gap, and the next evidence or review action. A supervisor can request revision without treating a learner’s work as a formal failure.

> **Non-gating safeguard:** Competency scores are developmental field-readiness feedback. They do not modify 80% module assessments, sequential access, certificate issuance, or owner alerts. High-consequence crop-protection advice remains bounded by current labels, authorised channels, laboratory, extension, or specialist confirmation.

## Privacy and data design

Each submission is owned by one authenticated learner and stored with a module ID, learner-authored evidence, a review status, optional supervisor attribution, scorecard, feedback, and timestamps. The supervisor queue is restricted to administrators. New feedback resets its private read state for future learner-notification integration, but no learner or supervisor can access another learner’s private evidence through the ordinary learner view.
