# Peer-Feedback Reflections

**Status:** Implemented learner-development workflow.  
**Author:** Manus AI  
**Purpose:** Help learners turn completed paired Field Inquiry feedback into a private, reviewable plan for a revised action and stronger evidence.

## Workflow

After one paired peer review is completed, the decision owner can open a private reflection within the same Field Inquiry workspace. The reflection is available only to the learner who owns the Field Inquiry and is linked to the completed peer-share record. The learner can save and later update one reflection for that completed review.

| Prompt | Learner purpose | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| **What I took from the peer feedback** | Identify a useful insight, question, or evidence gap. | Focus on learning and evidence, not peer judgement. |
| **What I will revise or test** | State the inquiry, interpretation, or next-action change to test. | Keep actions bounded and evidence-led. |
| **Evidence I will gather next** | Name an observation, comparison, record, or authorised source check. | Use referral when uncertainty remains. |

## Privacy and safeguards

The learner’s reflection is owner-scoped and can be retrieved or changed only through their authenticated learning account. A reflection cannot be created merely from a share ID: the associated peer review must already be completed and the requester must own that Field Inquiry decision. The peer cannot view or edit the owner’s reflection through the shared review link.

> **Non-gating boundary:** Peer-feedback reflections are voluntary learner sense-making. They do not change lesson completion, formal assessment scores, the 80% pass rule, sequential gates, competency scoring, certification, or owner alerts.

The reflection maintains the programme’s crop-safety boundary. It is not a place to create product, rate, threshold, medical, cleanup, disposal, legal-reporting, or unverified local-requirement advice. Learners should use current labels, authorised channels, laboratory, extension, or specialist referral where those checks are necessary.
